# TikTok Affiliate in the Philippines: A Plain Checklist for Your First ₱10,000

*By [MD Fozla Rabbi](https://mdfozlarabbi.smartstyledecor.com), SEO Specialist*

Most Filipino creators chase views first and money later. Affiliate flips that. You can earn with a small account if the right people watch. Here's the checklist I wish someone had handed me.

## 1. Pick products you'd actually use

Viewers can smell a fake review. Choose things you already own or can test at home. Kitchen tools, phone accessories, skincare, school supplies. Cheap items under ₱500 convert best because buyers don't overthink them.

## 2. Show the problem first

Don't open with the product. Open with the annoying problem. A tangled charger cable. A sticky frying pan. Then reveal the fix. That's the moment people tap the yellow basket.

## 3. Post when buyers scroll

Payday weekends (15th and 30th) and evenings after 8 PM tend to bring more orders. Plan your best videos around those days.

## 4. Keep a simple tracker

Write down every video: product, views, clicks, orders, commission. After 20 videos you'll see patterns. Some product types will clearly win. Do more of those.

## 5. Know the eligibility rules

TikTok Shop affiliate access has follower and age requirements and they change from time to time. Check them before you plan your month.

For current requirements, commission ranges and a step-by-step setup for local creators, this [TikTok affiliate Philippines guide](https://boostingph.com/blog/tiktok-affiliate-philippines) is clear and up to date.

## Quick recap

- Products under ₱500 that you really use
- Problem first, product second
- Post around payday
- Track every video

Small account. Real sales. That's the point of affiliate.
