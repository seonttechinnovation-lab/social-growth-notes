# SMM Panel India: A One-Page Buyer's Glossary

*By [MD Fozla Rabbi](https://mdfozlarabbi.smartstyledecor.com), SEO Specialist*

![SMM panel India buyer's glossary](img/yodha-gh.png)

Open any SMM panel's services page for the first time and it reads like a different language. "HQ, 0–1h start, 30D refill, drip-feed available, min 100, max 50K." Most Indian buyers just pick the cheapest line and hope.

This page fixes that. Every term you'll see, in plain words, with what it means for your money. Bookmark it. Come back when a service line confuses you.

## The basics

- **SMM panel.** A website where you order social media services (followers, likes, views, comments, watch time) by quantity. You paste a link, pick a number, pay and it delivers.
- **Service ID.** The number for one specific service. Useful when you talk to support or use the API.
- **Rate / price per 1,000.** What 1,000 units cost. ₹120 per 1,000 followers means 500 followers costs ₹60.
- **Min / max order.** The smallest and largest quantity you can order in one go. A low minimum lets you test cheaply.

## Delivery terms

- **Start time.** How long until delivery begins. "0–1h" is fast. "0–24h" means be patient.
- **Speed.** How many units arrive per day. 1K/day looks natural. 50K/day on a 300-follower account looks strange.
- **Drip-feed.** Splitting one order into smaller runs over time. 1,000 likes as 10 runs of 100, every few hours. Looks more organic.
- **Instant.** Delivers as fast as possible. Fine for views. Riskier for followers on small accounts.
- **Partial.** The order didn't fully complete. The unused amount is usually refunded to your balance.

## Quality terms

- **HQ / high quality.** Accounts with profile photos and some posts. Better looking. Not a guarantee they stay.
- **Real / active.** Marketing word for accounts that look more human. Always test.
- **Mixed.** A blend of quality levels. Usually cheaper.
- **Targeted / Indian.** Accounts from a specific country. Costs more. Useful if your audience is Indian and you care how the follower list looks.
- **Non-drop / low-drop.** Claims fewer losses over time. Test before trusting.

## After-delivery terms

- **Drop.** When delivered followers or likes disappear later. Normal to some degree with almost every panel.
- **Drop rate.** The percentage lost. 1,000 delivered and 850 left after a week is a 15% drop.
- **Refill.** Free top-up of dropped units within a window. **30D refill** means 30 days. **No refill** means drops are your loss.
- **Refill button.** Some panels let you request a refill yourself from order history. Fast panels process it within a day.
- **Cancel.** Stopping an order that hasn't started. Not always available.

## Money terms

- **Balance / add funds.** You top up a wallet first, then order from it.
- **UPI.** Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, BharatPe. The easiest way for Indian buyers. Check the panel accepts it.
- **Rupee vs dollar pricing.** Some panels show USD. Conversion and fees make the real rupee cost higher than it looks.
- **Real cost per 1,000.** Price divided by what stayed. A cheap service with a big drop can cost more than a pricier one with refill.

## Reseller terms

- **API.** A way for software to place orders automatically. Resellers use it to connect their own website to a supplier panel.
- **API key.** Your secret password for the API. Never share it publicly.
- **Child panel.** A ready-made panel website you rent and connect to a supplier, under your own brand.
- **Reseller.** Someone who buys from a panel and sells to their own customers at a markup.

## Safety terms

- **Link only.** A proper panel never needs your password. Only your profile or post link.
- **Private account.** Most services won't deliver to private accounts. Make it public during delivery.
- **Ticket.** A support request. Test response time with a small question before a big order.

## Putting it together

Here's how to read a real service line now:

*"Instagram Followers | HQ | 0–1h | 20K/day | 30D refill | min 100"*

Starts within an hour. Up to 20,000 per day. Better-looking accounts. Drops refilled free for 30 days. You can test with just 100. That's a decent line to start with.
