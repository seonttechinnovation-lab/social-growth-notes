# Bangla YouTube Channel Setup Checklist: 12 Things to Fix Before Chasing Subscribers

*By [MD Fozla Rabbi](https://mdfozlarabbi.smartstyledecor.com), SEO Specialist*

A lot of Bangladeshi creators start a YouTube channel, upload five videos, get 23 subscribers and then go hunting for subscriber tricks. But very often the problem isn't the videos. It's the channel setup. Visitors land on the channel page, see something confusing and leave without subscribing.

Here's a checklist to fix the basics first. Most of it takes one evening.

## Channel page basics

1. **A clear channel name.** If you teach spoken English in Bangla, say so. "Rafi Vlogs" tells nobody anything.
2. **Profile picture that works at tiny size.** Your face or a simple logo. Not a full poster.
3. **Banner with one promise and upload days.** "New Bangla tech reviews every Friday."
4. **About section with keywords.** Write how people would search: "Bangla cooking recipes," "HSC physics in Bangla."
5. **Channel trailer for new visitors.** 30 to 60 seconds. Who you are, what they'll get, why subscribe.

## Video-level fixes

6. **Titles people would actually type.** Mix Bangla and English the way your audience searches.
7. **Thumbnails readable on a phone.** Three or four big words max. High contrast.
8. **Hook in the first 15 seconds.** Show what the video delivers before your intro.
9. **Chapters on longer videos.** Viewers stay when they can jump to what they need.
10. **End screen to your best related video.** Keep people on your channel.

## Growth habits

11. **Playlists by topic.** A viewer who binges a playlist is far more likely to subscribe.
12. **Shorts that point to long videos.** Use Shorts for discovery and long videos for watch time.
